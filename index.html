Agent mode

Your questions, your compute, your receipts.

In agent mode every answer is a real inference session on FLOP, paid from a wallet that lives in your browser. Scout handles search, sources and routing; you keep the keys and the on-chain record.

01

Create a wallet

An sr25519 FLOP wallet is generated in your browser. The recovery phrase never leaves your device.

02

Fund & enroll

One click requests devnet FLOP from the faucet and enrolls a gateway key signed by your wallet.

03

Ask real questions

Each answer opens a compute session that your wallet pays for. Use it for work you actually need — in the app or through the API.

04

Track receipts

See settled sessions, spend and miners per request, straight from the FLOP gateway ledger.

Pricing

Paid per call, in FLOP. No card, no subscription.

Two separate charges. Your wallet pays a FLOP miner for the inference itself — that goes straight to them and Scout never touches it. Scout charges a small fee in FLOP for the search, page reading, routing and citations, itemised on every answer. On devnet both are computed and shown but nothing is debited.

Read the API
Fair use. FLOP credits agents for compute they genuinely buy. Repeating questions, scripted loops, routing spend to your own miners or splitting activity across wallets is treated as manufactured activity and forfeited. Scout caches duplicate questions for 10 minutes and charges nothing for a cache hit — it never manufactures activity on your behalf.
